\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\lead{Diffusion language models}
Early text diffusion operated in continuous embedding or simplex spaces \cite{li2022diffusionlm,han2023ssdlm}. Discrete masked diffusion simplifies training with reparameterized or Rao--Blackwellized objectives that approach AR perplexity \cite{zheng2023reparam,sahoo2024mdlm}, and idealised masked diffusion transformers match chain-of-thought expressivity \cite{svete2025reasoning}. LLaDA 8B trains masked diffusion from scratch \cite{nie2025llada}; continual pre-training \cite{ke2023continual} adapts AR checkpoints instead: DiffuLLaMA and DiffuGPT adapt GPT-2 and LLaMA-2 \cite{gong2025diffullama}, TESS~2 adapts an AR backbone \cite{tae2025tess2}, Dream 7B adapts Qwen2.5-7B \cite{ye2025dream7b}, and Dream-Coder targets code \cite{xie2025dreamcoder}. Repr-Align enforces layer-wise alignment during adaptation \cite{peng2026repralign}; we instead test whether unforced post-adaptation similarity predicts test-time AR reranking gain.

\lead{Test-time selection and verifiers}
Majority voting over samples (self-consistency; \cite{wang2023selfconsistency}) is a strong training-free baseline; trained verifiers rank GSM8K traces with supervised labels \cite{cobbe2021gsm8k}. DLM-specific methods vote across decoding block sizes (HEX; \cite{lee2025hex}), couple diffusion proposers with AR evaluators \cite{shao2025diffusethinking,berrayana2025planner}, or rerank with task-trained reward models (75.13\% vs.\ 60.05\% on Dream-Instruct; \cite{zhan2026prm}) and bidirectional consistency (BMC, 77.8\% vs.\ 74.3\% on LLaDA-Instruct; \cite{ruan2026bmc}). Those studies use Instruct checkpoints and different prompts, so their absolute scores are not comparable to our Base-checkpoint four-shot setting. Our selector needs no task-specific training, reads all candidates jointly, and handles both math and code.

\lead{Code evaluation and contamination}
HumanEval uses hidden unit tests \cite{chen2021humaneval}; EvalPlus (HumanEval+) expands coverage roughly $80\times$ to expose false passes \cite{liu2023evalplus}. Because GSM8K and HumanEval have been public since 2021, selectors can exploit answer familiarity; perturbation benchmarks such as GSM-Symbolic \cite{mirzadeh2024gsmsymbolic} motivate our controls and fresh-data follow-up.
