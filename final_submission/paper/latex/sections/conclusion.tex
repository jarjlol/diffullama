\section{Conclusion}

Majority vote is a strong baseline for choosing among generated solutions. The agent-designed rule matches it within 0.3 points. Reranking trails voting at 64 steps. At 256 steps the gap is smaller on Dream, and the confidence interval on LLaDA includes zero. Voting also adds fewer points over the first solution at 256 steps.

The results leave open whether a diffusion model benefits more from reranking when its activations resemble those of its AR base. When we limit scoring to text through the final answer, reranking changes from hurting Dream to helping it relative to the first solution. The four-model result remains descriptive because the smallest attainable two-sided exact permutation $p$ is 0.083. Further work needs a controlled cost comparison, a 256-step run on all 500 questions, tests that vary one setting at a time, and a second dataset.
