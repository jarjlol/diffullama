\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across five DLMs, AR likelihood trails majority by over 20 points at 64 steps and still loses at 256, while cutting trailing text shifts Dream by 13 points and CKA does not predict gain. A 4B decision model beats majority by 5.2 points at 64 (78.0\% vs.\ 72.8\%) and 4.0 at 256 (85.7\% vs.\ 81.7\%; diff-in-diff +0.5, $p=0.82$), reaching 78.5\% with 47.8\% of calls under weak-vote gating; on code it hits the 40.3\% HumanEval oracle and 51/53 HumanEval+. Controls bound the claim to selection, not verified reasoning; fresh benchmarks remain next steps.
