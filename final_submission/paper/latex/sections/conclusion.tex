\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across five DLMs, AR likelihood trails majority by more than 20 points at 64 steps and still loses at 256. Cutting trailing text shifts Dream by 13 points, and CKA does not predict gain. A 4B decision model beats majority by 5.2 points at 64 (78.0\% vs.\ 72.8\%) and by 4.0 at 256 (85.7\% vs.\ 81.7\%; difference-in-differences +0.5, $p=0.82$). With weak-vote gating it reaches 78.5\% using 47.8\% of calls. On code it hits the 40.3\% HumanEval oracle and selects 51/53 HumanEval+ solvable tasks. Controls bound the claim to selection, not verified reasoning. Fresh benchmarks remain the next step.
