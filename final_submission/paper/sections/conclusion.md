# Conclusion

In the completed fixed-candidate evaluation, majority vote is a strong baseline: 73.3% for Dream and 73.0% for LLaDA on 300 held-out questions. The agent-designed arithmetic rule matches it within 0.3 points, and AR-likelihood selection remains 23.6 and 21.0 points below majority on the 500-question cut-text test. The preservation hypothesis is not confirmed: the original scores gave the predicted rank order, but the corrected scores reverse Dream's sign, and a two-sided exact permutation $p$ of at least 0.083 leaves the four-model result descriptive at best. Step-budget runs show that at 16 steps more denoising adds far more accuracy than selection, while at 64 steps majority adds 32.0 and 24.6 points over the first sample. A conclusive next study needs corrected scoring at every budget, a clean compute comparison, ablations and a second dataset. We report these as requirements, not as completed results.
