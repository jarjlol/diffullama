Review:
The proposed method exhibits a clear logical structure and directly addresses the research question through a well-defined pipeline: measuring representation preservation, isolating reranking versus denoising gains, and testing their relationship. However, it suffers from critical threats to internal and statistical validity that undermine its ability to draw reliable conclusions. The primary concern is the severe confounding between model architecture and preservation scores: using GPT-2-small as the universal AR scorer systematically biases results, as GPT-2-based DLMs (DiffuGPT) will inherently show higher preservation and better scorer alignment than LLaMA-based models, regardless of actual representation fidelity. With only five models (n=5), the statistical analyses—including Spearman correlations, OLS regression, and bootstrap CIs—are severely underpowered, making the claimed moderator effects (GPT-2 vs. LLaMA families) uninterpretable. Furthermore, the construct validity of CKA (linear kernel) as a measure of "AR-specific representation preservation" is questionable; high geometric similarity does not guarantee that the preserved features are those utilized by the AR scorer. The Unified Quality Score (simple average across heterogeneous benchmarks) assumes equal interval scaling and equal importance, which may obscure domain-specific effects. While the ablation plan is comprehensive, it does not resolve the fundamental power and confounding issues.

Feedback:
1. **Resolve scorer confounding**: Use architecture-matched scorers as the primary analysis (GPT-2-small for GPT-2-based DLMs, LLaMA-7B for LLaMA-based DLMs) and treat scorer-family match as a covariate or stratify the analysis, rather than relying on a single cross-family scorer.
2. **Acknowledge statistical limitations**: With n=5, abandon hypothesis testing in favor of descriptive effect sizes and confidence intervals interpreted as uncertainty bounds, not significance tests. Pre-register the analysis as exploratory and emphasize replication with additional checkpoints.
3. **Validate the preservation construct**: Include a behavioral check—verify that preservation scores correlate with the DLM's ability to mimic AR model outputs on a held-out set, not just geometric similarity. Consider using representation editing or causal mediation analysis to test whether preservation actually *causes* reranking gains.
4. **Refine the quality metric**: Replace the simple average UQS with benchmark-specific effect sizes or use PCA-derived weights only if validated on an independent set; report results both ways to check sensitivity.
5. **Address diminishing returns**: The efficiency ratio ε assumes linear scaling of denoising steps; verify this by testing non-linear fits or using the actual marginal gain per step from the data.
