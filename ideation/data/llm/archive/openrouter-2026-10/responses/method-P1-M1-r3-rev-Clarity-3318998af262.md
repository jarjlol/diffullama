 Review: The method is exceptionally well-organized and detailed, with precise mathematical definitions for the quality metric, compute efficiency, and Pareto construction. The logical flow from model preparation through trade-off analysis is easy to follow, and the explicit hyperparameters and reproducibility package (Docker, seeds, profiling logs) strongly support replication. The fixed-reference normalization for the unified quality metric is a particularly clear design choice that avoids circularity.

Feedback: While the method is highly detailed, the adaptive two-stage allocation strategy (Section 4) involves nested conditional logic and equations (e.g., the ΔS budget distribution) that would benefit from pseudocode or a simple flowchart to aid comprehension. The tokenizer fallback procedure and the dense generalizability subsections (Section 7) could be moved to supplementary material or summarized with bullet points to keep the core protocol focused. Additionally, clarifying the diffusion “continue denoising” step for readers unfamiliar with iterative refinement, and explicitly defining all variables in the FLOP-equivalence equations, would eliminate minor ambiguities and make the method accessible to a broader computational audience.

Rating (1-5): 4
