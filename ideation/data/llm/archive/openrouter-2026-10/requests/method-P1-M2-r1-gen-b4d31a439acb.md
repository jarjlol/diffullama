While informative, this approach conflates two distinct notions of preservation: (i) how well the DLM’s *hidden‑state geometry* matches the AR geometry, and (ii) how well the DLM’s *output likelihood* aligns with the AR distribution.  

ReGUIDE decouples these ideas by **(1)** measuring preservation as the *subspace alignment* between DLM and AR hidden‑state spaces—a training‑free, geometry‑centric metric that directly quantifies the extent to which the DLM reuses the AR representational basis. **(2)** It then reranks candidates not by AR likelihood but by the *procrustes similarity* between the DLM’s hidden states (produced during denoising) and the AR model’s hidden states for the same candidate.
