Review: The method is exceptionally well-structured, with a step-by-step table that provides concrete actions, resources, and constraints. Mathematical definitions for preservation scores, gains, and efficiency ratios are precise, and the ablation plan is comprehensive. Feedback: Three minor clarity gaps remain: (1) Hidden-state extraction is described as "collect the hidden-state tensor" but the mechanism (forward hooks / intermediate outputs) is not specified, which is critical for replication; (2) Candidate generation uses a "same global random seed across all models," but for diffusion sampling it is unclear whether each candidate draws independent noise or shares noise seeds across models—this affects diversity and comparability; (3) The probing corpus construction ("10k sentences randomly sampled from WikiText-103 + C4") is ambiguous because C4 is document-level and sentence segmentation is not specified. Additionally, with only five models, the bootstrap resampling unit for Spearman CIs should be explicitly stated (prompts vs. models). These do not undermine the design but are needed for straightforward replication.
