Review: The research problem investigates how inference-time attention-masking strategies (causal, bidirectional, hybrid) affect the quality-efficiency trade-off of diffusion language models adapted from autoregressive checkpoints, across model families and scales. The problem is well-motivated, clearly articulated, and feasible. However, its originality is constrained by the fact that the core premise—comparing attention masking strategies for diffusion language models—has already been substantially explored in related work, particularly PreDiff-LM (which directly compared hybrid vs. bidirectional attention for diffusion models adapted from AR checkpoints) and the target paper itself (which discusses hybrid attention and mask annealing). The proposed study would provide a more systematic and comprehensive empirical analysis, but the fundamental question is not entirely unexplored.

Feedback: The problem's main strength is its practical significance and clear experimental design. However, for originality, several concerns arise: (1) The idea that attention masking influences diffusion LM performance has already been demonstrated in PreDiff-LM, which showed hybrid attention improves perplexity from 34.1 to 28.7 over bidirectional attention; (2) The target paper already discusses and partially explores attention-mask annealing and hybrid attention during training; (3) The problem essentially asks "what is the optimal inference-time mask?" which is an empirical optimization question rather than a novel theoretical or methodological challenge. To strengthen originality, the problem could be reframed to address a less-explored angle—for instance, how inference-time attention masking interacts with *different adaptation objectives* (uniform-noise vs. masked diffusion), or whether mask choice reveals fundamental limitations of AR-to-diffusion conversion that cannot be resolved by training-time adjustments alone. As stated, the problem offers a thorough empirical investigation but does not introduce a sufficiently novel challenge or perspective to stand out from existing work.
