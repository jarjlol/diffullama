Review: The method presents a logically organized, comprehensive framework that directly addresses the research problem through systematic manipulation of denoising steps and candidate counts, grounded in rigorous compute normalization. The structure facilitates comprehension, and the statistical approach (bootstrap resampling, Pareto frontier analysis) is appropriate for the questions posed.

Feedback: While the static experimental design is clearly specified, the adaptive allocation strategy (Section 4) lacks precise algorithmic detail regarding how the "remaining compute budget" is calculated and distributed across candidates, making exact replication ambiguous. The generalizability checks (Section 7) remain too vague for replication—specific held-out benchmarks and alternative DLM checkpoints should be enumerated rather than referenced generically. Additionally, the assumption that AR NLL serves as a universal quality proxy across architecturally distinct models (GPT-2 vs. LLaMA-based diffusion models) requires empirical validation, and the tokenizer swap procedure (Section 1) needs explicit justification regarding potential semantic misalignment between diffusion and AR tokenizers.

Rating (1-5): 4
