Review: The research problem is clearly relevant to the current landscape of diffusion language modeling and builds directly on the target paper's findings. It identifies a genuine, under-examined dimension—inference-time attention masking strategy—that has been touched upon at the training level (notably in PreDiff-LM, which demonstrated hybrid attention improves perplexity from 34.1 to 28.7) but has not been systematically explored at inference time on fixed adapted checkpoints. The problem is well-structured, with a clear rationale connecting to the target paper's reported limitations (shift operations, mask annealing, [MASK] token handling) and to related works such as Jacobi Forcing (pretrain-to-posttrain mismatch) and UNIFUSION (objective connections across diffusion formulations). The feasibility argument is solid, leveraging released checkpoints and inference-only experimentation, and the significance is articulated across scientific, practical, and broader-impact dimensions.

Feedback: The problem is well-defined and the experimental design is sound. A few suggestions to strengthen it: (1) The rationale's references to "shift operation (L2)" and "attention-mask annealing (L1)" would benefit from brief explicit definitions for readers unfamiliar with the target paper's internal ablations, as these terms are not self-explanatory in isolation. (2) The problem could more explicitly acknowledge that PreDiff-LM (#9) has already studied hybrid vs. bidirectional attention during *training*, and clarify precisely how the proposed inference-time investigation differs and adds value beyond what PreDiff-LM addressed—this would preempt concerns about incremental overlap. (3) The inclusion of model-family comparison (GPT-2-based vs. LLaMA-based) is a strong differentiator that should be emphasized more, as it addresses whether AR-to-diffusion transfer properties are architecture-dependent. (4) The timeline and resource estimates, while detailed, could be slightly tempered—the claim that a single GPU suffices may not account for the memory requirements of 7B-scale models during batched inference with bidirectional attention patterns, which could require gradient checkpointing or model offloading strategies.
