Review: The method presents a systematic 8-phase experimental framework with clearly defined metrics (UQM, FLOPs, AUPC) and statistical validation (bootstrap). However, the core AR-MHDS algorithm raises theoretical concerns regarding detailed balance and the discrete-continuous mismatch between diffusion noise trajectories and AR token-level scoring.

Feedback: While the structure is rigorous, the Metropolis-Hastings formulation requires clarification: (1) the proposal kernel (Gaussian perturbation of noise vectors followed by diffusion denoising) likely violates detailed balance with respect to the AR-likelihood target, risking non-convergence to the desired distribution; (2) the discrete nature of text generation conflicts with continuous noise-space proposals without explicit discretization handling or acceptance ratio correction; (3) convergence diagnostics (burn-in, mixing, autocorrelation) are absent despite the Markov chain structure; (4) the FLOP model (α≈2) is approximate and ignores memory-bound latency effects that dominate on modern GPUs; (5) the comparison baseline confounds correlation (MH chains) with independence, complicating the efficiency comparison. Addressing these theoretical and practical gaps would strengthen the rigorousness.
