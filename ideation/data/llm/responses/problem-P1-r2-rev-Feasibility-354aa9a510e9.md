Review: The research problem is well-formulated and directly addresses a gap at the intersection of representation alignment (REPR-ALIGN, PreDiff-LM) and inference-time efficiency methods (Jacobi Forcing, TESS 2). It proposes a clear, multi-stage methodology—measuring AR-representation preservation via CKA/linear probing, generating candidates across step budgets, reranking with a lightweight scorer, and correlating preservation quality with selection gains—that is technically sound and grounded in the existing literature. The inference-only constraint eliminates the most prohibitive computational bottleneck, and the use of released checkpoints is well-aligned with available resources.

However, several feasibility concerns warrant attention. (1) **Generation throughput on a single 96GB GPU** is the primary bottleneck: running iterative denoising at 16–128 steps across five model families (including a 6.74B DiffuLLaMA) with multiple candidates per prompt is computationally expensive and could consume the majority of the 10-week window. Quantization or model offloading strategies may be needed to maintain viable throughput. (2) **CKA computation between full 6.74B and AR base representations** requires simultaneous forward passes through both models, which may exceed 96GB unless activations are carefully managed or subsampled. (3) **Access to all five checkpoints** (especially DiffuLLaMA at 6.74B and Dream-7B) is assumed but not guaranteed—any licensing or access restriction would force redesign. (4) The **correlation analysis** (step 5) is conceptually elegant but depends critically on sufficient statistical power, which requires enough prompt-model-step combinations; this ties back to the generation throughput constraint.

Despite these concerns, the problem is solvable within the stated constraints through careful prioritization (e.g., focusing on 3–4 models rather than all 5, reducing candidate counts, or limiting step budgets to {32, 64, 128}), leveraging the 7-person team for parallel CPU-bound evaluation and analysis work, and using the well-established benchmark harnesses mentioned.

Feedback: The problem is strong in its conceptual clarity, grounding in existing literature, and methodological rigor. The main actionable feedback is to (a) explicitly plan for GPU memory management strategies (activation checkpointing, quantization) for the 6.74B DiffuLLaMA model, (b) pre-validate access to all five checkpoints early in the timeline, (c) consider a prioritized model subset (e.g., DiffuGPT-S/M, DiffuLLaMA, Dream-7B) to ensure sufficient generation throughput within 10 weeks, and (d) pre-build and CPU-test all evaluation harnesses (HumanEval, GSM8K, SIQA/WinoGrande) before GPU-dependent generation begins. The study's value in bridging representation-alignment theory with inference-time efficiency is significant and would benefit from a slightly more conservative scope to ensure thorough execution.
