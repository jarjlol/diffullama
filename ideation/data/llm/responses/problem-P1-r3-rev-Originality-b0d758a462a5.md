Review:
The research problem is well-structured and methodologically rigorous, with clear evaluation protocols and a feasible experimental plan. However, its originality is questionable upon close inspection against the existing literature. The core investigation—how to allocate inference compute in DLMs between denoising steps and candidate selection—overlaps substantially with several prior works: Dream 7B already explores "tunable quality-speed trade-offs" and inference flexibility; Diffuse Thinking explicitly combines DLMs as proposal generators with AR models as evaluators; UNIFUSION systematically studies the effect of denoising step budgets (16–256 steps) on quality metrics; and Jacobi Forcing addresses the AR-to-parallel decoding efficiency gap. The proposed Pareto frontier analysis is a sound methodology, but it constitutes a systematic empirical comparison of already-known design axes rather than identifying a genuinely new challenge or phenomenon. The lightweight AR reranker idea is also standard practice in AR LLMs (nucleus sampling, reranking) and has been applied to DLMs before. The problem's contribution is primarily organizational and quantitative (explicit compute normalization, direct head-to-head comparison of S vs. N investment), rather than conceptual or methodological novelty.

Feedback: To strengthen originality, the problem could be reframed around a specific gap or unexpected phenomenon revealed by preliminary results—for instance, if the Pareto analysis uncovers a previously unrecognized regime where candidate diversity matters more than denoising depth (or vice versa), or if the lightweight reranker exposes a fundamental limitation of current DLM architectures. As stated, the problem reads as a thorough benchmarking study with clear practical value but limited conceptual novelty. Consider adding a hypothesis-driven element (e.g., a specific prediction about the shape of the Pareto frontier or an interaction effect between S and N) that could be falsified by the experiment, which would elevate it from empirical characterization to genuine contribution.

Rating (1-5): 3
